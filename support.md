## Getting Started

**How do I add players and build a team?**
Open **Team Builder** from the settings bar, add your players, and set your team names. Then use **Setup Builder** to create a formation with named positions, and drag players onto the field.

**How do I start the game clock?**
Tap the **play button** next to the clock to start. The clock pulses for alerts and can be corrected at any time from Timer Options if you forget to pause or resume.

## Substitutions & Playing Time

**How do I make a substitution?**
- **Move a player:** tap a player, then tap an open spot.
- **Sub from the bench:** tap a bench player, then tap a position.
- **Swap two players:** long-press, two-finger swap, or drag one onto another.
- **Stage several changes** and commit them all at once with the checkmark.

**What do the colored meters mean?**
Each player card shows live time meters: total minutes played, current on-field stint, and bench rest time. The stint indicator flashes when a player reaches your sub interval so no one gets forgotten on the field.

**What is Sub History?**
Every change is logged with the game time. Open the Sub History drawer to flash back through your substitutions and see who came on for whom.

## Alerts

**How do I change or turn off alerts?**
In **Timer Options** you can set the sub-alert interval, schedule hydration breaks, enable a half-time alert, exclude the goalie from alerts, and turn vibrations off entirely (the clock still pulses and cards still flash).

## Apple Watch

**The watch isn't showing my game.**
Make sure the Watch app is installed and your watch is paired and nearby. The watch mirrors the currently active game — open the game on your iPhone first, then check your wrist. If it still doesn't sync, relaunch both apps.

## Sharing

**How does Live Share work?**
Live Share mirrors your game to nearby devices on the same Wi-Fi network. Tap the antenna icon to host, and the game appears on the other device.

**How do I send a saved game to another coach?**
In Saved Game States, tap the share button on a saved game. The app creates an encrypted `.smgame` file that you can send by AirDrop, Mail, or Messages.

**The shared file won't open when I tap it in Messages.**
Because the file is encrypted, it can't be opened straight from the Messages conversation. Instead:
1. **Save the file first** — tap it, then choose **Save to Files** (or use AirDrop, which opens the app directly).
2. Open the **Files** app and tap the saved `.smgame` file.
3. Soccer Manager Pro launches and asks you to confirm the import.

Tip: **AirDrop** is the quickest way to share between two devices — the receiving device opens the app and prompts to import automatically.

## Data & Privacy

**Where is my data stored?**
Everything stays on your device. The app has no account and collects no data. See our Privacy Policy.

**I got a new phone — how do I move my data?**
Use **Share** on a saved game to send it to your new device, or restore your device from an iCloud/encrypted backup, which includes the app's local data.
